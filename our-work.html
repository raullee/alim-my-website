Events & Community Engagement

Our commitment to grassroots education and community building through panel discussions, workshops, and cultural events.

Featured Event

Art & Law Panel Discussion

September 27, 2025 | 1:00 PM

A landmark panel discussion featuring Dato' Tiffanee Marie Lim, Marisa R. Ng, and Fahri Azzat, exploring legal protections and rights for Malaysia's artistic community. The event also marked the launch of Project Sumandak: CIPELA's Heritage Collection, crafted with Kain Dastar by Sabah's women artisans.

July 2025

ARTXLAW Workshop Series

Sundays in July | 4-6 PM | Estudio Trauma Center

Co-founders Raul Lee, Fahri Azzat, and Nicholas Choong leading intensive workshops on artist rights, contracts, and legal literacy for practicing musicians and artists.

Workshop Musicians
September 2025

Bilingual Legal Learning Sessions

Sept 10 (8-10 PM) & Sept 27 (3-5 PM) | Rumah Attap Library & Collective

Legal learning and advice sessions conducted in English and Bahasa Malaysia, ensuring accessibility across diverse creative communities. Focus on contract law, IP protection, and dispute resolution.

Bilingual Inclusive
October 2025

ART X FASHION Hangout

October 18, 2025 | 2-5 PM | Greydea Studio, Taman Tun Dr Ismail

An open community space for fashion creatives to network, seek advice, draft patterns, and learn about their legal rights in a relaxed, collaborative environment.

Fashion Networking
Cultural Partnership

Hari Kopek Performance

October 17-18, 2025 | Performance 8:30 PM | REX KL

Supporting the All-Female Multinational Wayang Kulit/Tatung Show, demonstrating our commitment to protecting and empowering artists across all cultural mediums. Featured Q&A session on artists' rights and performance contracts.

Supported by the Krishen Jit Fund, this groundbreaking show brought together female performers from Thailand, Malaysia, Cambodia, Myanmar, Indonesia, and the Netherlands.
