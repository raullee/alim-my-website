Service Tax Act 2018

SERVICE TAX ACT 2018

AKTA CUKAI PERKHIDMATAN 2018

Act 807 · consolidated to 1 Jan 2025 · 111 pp

Socio-economic rights
Consolidated to 1 January 2025. Amendments made after that date are not in this text. Check the AGC record for later amendments.
03
Why it matters

What this does to artists

Service tax reaches a number of arts-adjacent supplies once the registration threshold is crossed, and the scope has been widened repeatedly, most recently in 2025. Relevant to agencies, venues, production services and digital services.

Watch this

Thresholds and the taxable-services list change by order almost every year. Check the current First Schedule, not a remembered figure.
