Artistic freedom

03
Why it matters

What this does to artists

The general criminal law, and the home of the provisions most often turned on artists: obscenity (ss.292 to 294, which reach obscene objects, books, drawings and songs or recitations in public), wounding religious feelings (s.298 and s.298A), and statements conducing to public mischief (s.505).

Watch this

Section 294(a) reaches an obscene song, ballad or words in or near a public place. It is the busking and stand-up provision.
