National Heritage Act 2005

NATIONAL HERITAGE ACT 2005

AKTA WARISAN KEBANGSAAN 2005

Act 645 · consolidated to 1 Jun 2006 · 76 pp

Artistic freedomSocio-economic rights
This copy of the Act is 20 years old. Any amendment made since 1 June 2006 is not in this text. Check the AGC record for later amendments.
03
Why it matters

What this does to artists

Heritage listing, national heritage objects, and controls on the export and alteration of heritage items. It reaches artists working with heritage material and sites, conservators, and any museum or archive acquiring or lending.

Watch this

Declaration constrains what an owner may do with their own property, and objects can be caught on export without anyone realising they were listed.
