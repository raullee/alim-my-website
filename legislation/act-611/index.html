Child Act 2001

CHILD ACT 2001

AKTA KANAK-KANAK 2001

Act 611 · consolidated to 1 Jul 2023 · 148 pp

Socio-economic rights
This copy of the Act is 3 years old. Any amendment made since 1 July 2023 is not in this text. Check the AGC record for later amendments.
03
Why it matters

What this does to artists

Child performers. Anyone casting, filming or staging a child, and anyone putting a child on a channel, is inside this Act. It also carries the reporting duties that apply if a child on a production is at risk.
