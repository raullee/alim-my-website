Contracts Act 1950

CONTRACTS ACT 1950

AKTA KONTRAK 1950

Act 136 · consolidated to 1 Jan 2006 · 91 pp

Socio-economic rights
This copy of the Act is 20 years old. Any amendment made since 1 January 2006 is not in this text. Check the AGC record for later amendments.
03
Why it matters

What this does to artists

Every commission, gig, residency, publishing deal, sync licence and band agreement is a contract, and this is the Act that decides whether one exists, what it means and what happens when it is broken. Read with the Copyright Act for any deal touching rights.

Watch this

Section 28 makes agreements in restraint of trade void, which is why most exclusivity clauses in Malaysian artist and management agreements are weaker than they look.
