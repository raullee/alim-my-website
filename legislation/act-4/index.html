Employees' Social Security Act 1969

EMPLOYEES' SOCIAL SECURITY ACT 1969

AKTA KESELAMATAN SOSIAL PEKERJA 1969

Act 4 · consolidated to 1 Oct 2024 · 177 pp

Socio-economic rights
Consolidated to 1 October 2024. Amendments made after that date are not in this text. Check the AGC record for later amendments.
03
Why it matters

What this does to artists

SOCSO for employees. Relevant to arts organisations, venues, galleries and production companies as employers, and to any artist who is in truth an employee.
