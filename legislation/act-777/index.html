Why it matters

What this does to artists

The vehicle statute. Sdn Bhd for a production company or label, and the company limited by guarantee in s.45 for an arts non-profit. It is the form ALIM intends to take.

Watch this

A CLBG needs the Minister's licence under s.45 to drop 'Berhad', and it cannot pay dividends. That is the trade-off for the charitable form.
