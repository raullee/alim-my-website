Children and Young Persons (Employment) Act 1966

CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS (EMPLOYMENT) ACT 1966

AKTA KANAK-KANAK DAN ORANG MUDA (PEKERJAAN) 1966

Act 350 · consolidated to 10 Jan 2019 · 24 pp

Socio-economic rights
This copy of the Act is 7 years old. Any amendment made since 10 January 2019 is not in this text. Check the AGC record for later amendments.
03
Why it matters

What this does to artists

The specific permission regime for employing children and young persons, including in public entertainment. A child's appearance in a film, show or paid performance needs to be squared with this Act, and the parent's consent is not the whole answer.

Watch this

Public entertainment is separately regulated here; do not rely on a parental release alone for a paid child performer.
