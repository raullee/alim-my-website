Gig Workers Act 2025

GIG WORKERS ACT 2025

AKTA PEKERJA GIG 2025

Act 872 · consolidated to 31 Dec 2025 · 59 pp

Socio-economic rights
Consolidated to 31 December 2025. Amendments made after that date are not in this text. Check the AGC record for later amendments.
03
Why it matters

What this does to artists

The first Malaysian statute written for people who are not employees, and in large part an arts statute. Its Schedule names the work directly: acting, film production crew, singers, musicians, composers, lyricists, make-up artists, hair stylists, stylists, interpreters, translators, transcribers, journalists, stringers, photographers and videographers. If that is what you do, you are a gig worker under this Act whether or not a platform is involved. It gives you a written service agreement, payment within seven days where the agreement is silent, no termination without just cause, free conciliation before the Director General for Industrial Relations, and a Tribunal.

Watch this

Two things the page cannot show you. First, s.33 gives the Tribunal power to make any award it thinks just and appropriate, including consequential loss, with no monetary ceiling written into it; small claims stops dead at RM5,000. Second, s.36(3) bars advocates and solicitors from appearing at all, but s.36(2)(b) lets a gig worker be represented by an official of a gig workers' association or by a family member. A gig workers' association means one registered under the Societies Act 1966, so a company limited by guarantee cannot be one. You have to reach the Tribunal through conciliation first; you cannot file there directly.
