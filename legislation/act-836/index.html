Geographical Indications Act 2022

GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATIONS ACT 2022

AKTA PETUNJUK GEOGRAFI 2022

Act 836 · consolidated to 16 Mar 2022 · 83 pp

Intellectual property rightsSocio-economic rights
This copy of the Act is 4 years old. Any amendment made since 16 March 2022 is not in this text. Check the AGC record for later amendments.
03
Why it matters

What this does to artists

The instrument for craft and heritage products tied to a place. Songket, batik, pua kumbu, Sarawak and Sabah craft traditions, regional foodstuffs. It protects a collective, place-based reputation rather than one producer's brand, which is the right shape for craft communities.

Watch this

Replaced the Geographical Indications Act 2000 (Act 602) on 18 March 2022.
