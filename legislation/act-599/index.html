Consumer Protection Act 1999

CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT 1999

AKTA PERLINDUNGAN PENGGUNA 1999

Act 599 · consolidated to 1 Nov 2021 · 137 pp

Socio-economic rights
This copy of the Act is 4 years old. Any amendment made since 1 November 2021 is not in this text. Check the AGC record for later amendments.
03
Why it matters

What this does to artists

Applies to artists as sellers, which any merch table, print drop or online store makes you. The e-commerce regulations impose mandatory disclosure of identity and terms on an online seller, and the Tribunal for Consumer Claims is a cheap route both for and against you.

Watch this

The Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transactions) Regulations 2012 require an online seller to display name, registration number and address. Most artist stores do not.
