Societies Act 1966 (Revised - 2021)

SOCIETIES ACT 1966 (REVISED - 2021)

AKTA PERTUBUHAN 1966 (DISEMAK - 2021)

Act 832 · consolidated to 15 Nov 2021 · 95 pp

Socio-economic rightsArtistic freedom
This copy of the Act is 4 years old. Any amendment made since 15 November 2021 is not in this text. Check the AGC record for later amendments.
03
Why it matters

What this does to artists

The other vehicle for an arts collective: a registered society. Cheaper and faster than a CLBG, but the Registrar has wide powers over registration, rules and dissolution, which is a live freedom-of-association issue for politically engaged arts groups.
