How We Help

A multi-faceted approach to support the creative ecosystem.

Confidential Legal Advice

Direct, one-on-one support for artists facing contract disputes, IP issues, and more.

Policy Advocacy

Working to reform laws and policies to create a fairer, more sustainable creative sector.

Impact Story

Protecting a Muralist's Right to Expression

When a public artist's work was threatened with removal due to a contractual misunderstanding, ALIM intervened. We provided pro-bono legal counsel, clarified the terms of the commission, and successfully mediated a solution that preserved the artwork and ensured the artist was fairly compensated.
