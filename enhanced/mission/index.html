The principles that guide everything we do at ALIM.

06
Foundational principles

The Six Pillars

01

Empower

Equip artists and cultural workers with the legal knowledge and confidence to protect their work and livelihoods.

02

Foster Understanding

Build mutual literacy between the creative and legal worlds so each speaks the other's language.

03

Encourage Harmony

Resolve disputes early and quietly, favouring dialogue over the courtroom wherever possible.

04

Promote Appreciation

Champion the cultural and economic value of creative work in law and policy.

05

Preserve

Safeguard Malaysia's creative heritage and the rights that keep it alive.

06

Disrupt with Intent

Challenge the norms and gaps that leave creatives unprotected, deliberately and constructively.

Be part of it

These pillars only stand because people like you hold them up.
