Three cases this quarter will reshape the legal terrain Malaysia's creative communities work on. ALIM is on watching brief for each. Help where you can.

Urgent — Mediation File № 01 · Music sector

Activist musicians v. MACP (defamation)

A group of activist musicians is facing a USD 2 million defamation suit from the Music Authors' Copyright Protection (MACP) collection management organisation, reportedly tied to public statements made while pushing for royalty-system reform. Mediation is imminent. Current representation is reported to be inexperienced for a claim of this scale.

Plaintiff
MACP (collection management organisation)
Defendants
Activist musicians (group, names withheld pending consent)
Quantum
≈ USD 2,000,000 in damages claimed
Stage
Pre-mediation
ALIM position
Seeking experienced defamation/media counsel to assist or take over. Coordinating with Karl & Fahri.
Risk if lost
Chilling effect on industry reform criticism; sets precedent for CMO retaliation

Watching brief — November File № 03 · Music royalties · Admin law

CMOs v. MyIPO (transparency JR)

MACP, PPM and RPM (the three big collection management organisations) have initiated a judicial review against MyIPO's new transparency guidelines for royalty collection. Activist musicians are exploring intervention. The court date is in November 2026.

Recent — for reference

Federal Court: moral rights are inalienable.

A recent Federal Court decision affirmed that an artist's moral rights do not transfer with the sale of copyright. The burden of proof and cost of enforcement, however, still make it impractical for most artists to defend these rights. ALIM is exploring a pro bono enforcement track for representative cases.
