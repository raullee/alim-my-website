Our services & engagement process.

How ALIM supports Malaysia's creative communities: confidential consultations, contract and document review, and dispute resolution support. Free. Private. Practical.

01
Core service

Confidential legal consultations

01

Case discussion

A private conversation about what happened and what you need, on your terms.

02

Rights & obligations

A clear read of where you stand under Malaysian law, in plain language.

03

Position assessment

An honest view of your leverage, your risks, and what a good outcome looks like.

04

Strategic options

The routes open to you, from a quiet letter to formal escalation, and what each costs.

Document services

Contract & document review

Commission & work-for-hire

Know what you are signing away before you sign, and what to keep.

Licensing & royalty contracts

Terms, scope, and payment read closely so the deal matches the promise.

Gallery representation

Representation agreements reviewed for the traps artists rarely see coming.

Non-disclosure agreements

NDAs checked for reach and duration before they bind you.

Conflict support

Dispute resolution support

Letters of demand

A firm, well-drafted letter often ends a dispute before it starts.

Negotiation strategy

A plan for the conversation, so you walk in knowing your lines and your limits.

Mediation support

Guidance through mediation, with your interests kept in the room.

Important limitations
  • We do not provide full court representation; we advise, guide, and refer.
  • We do not handle criminal, family, or property law matters.
  • Our support is advisory. The decisions, always, are yours.
