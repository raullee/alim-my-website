§ On purpose Alim is a legal clinic for Malaysia's creative communities. Independent. Non-profit. Run by volunteer counsel who believe the right to make is worth defending, first in private, then, if pressed, in public.

Art is protected speech.

Some lawyers think so too.

ALIM gives Malaysia's artists, musicians, designers, writers and filmmakers the confidential legal cover they're routinely denied. Free advice. Policy advocacy. Education. No retainer. No PR.

01 / Advice

Confidential legal advice

Free, private consultations on the problems creatives actually face: contracts, IP, performance and venue agreements, freelance disputes, and freedom of expression.

02 / Advocacy

Policy advocacy

We speak into the legal conversations that shape creative livelihoods, so the rules that govern making are written with makers in the room.

03 / Education

Educational resources

Plain-language guides, interactive tools, and workshops that put the law within reach, in English and Bahasa Malaysia.

Impact story

Protecting a muralist's right to expression.

When a public mural drew a takedown threat, ALIM stepped in with confidential advice and quiet mediation, no courtroom, no press. The work stayed up; the artist kept their rights. This is most of what we do: pressure resolved before it ever becomes a case.
