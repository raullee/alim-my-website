Alim is a legal clinic for Malaysia's creative communities. Independent. Non-profit. Unaffiliated with any studio, gallery, label, or publisher. Run by volunteer counsel who believe that the right to make is worth defending — first in private, then, if pressed, in court.

§ I The Charter
Filed 14 Aug 2024
“

Every artist deserves a lawyer. Not when they get sued — but the day they sign their first contract, paint their first wall, post their first track.

We exist because the gap between Malaysian creative practice and Malaysian creative law is too wide, too quiet, and too lopsided. Galleries have lawyers. Labels have lawyers. Studios have lawyers. The artist usually has Google.

We close that gap with three instruments: private counsel for the artist who needs it now; policy submissions when the law itself is the wound; and free education so the next generation never has to learn this through litigation.

None of this is charity. It is repair. A creative class that knows its rights is a country that can afford a culture. Malaysia can afford a culture. We are simply doing the bookkeeping.
