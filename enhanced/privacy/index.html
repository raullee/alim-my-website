Privacy policy.

§

ALIM (Art x Law Initiative Malaysia) treats your privacy and confidentiality with the seriousness they deserve. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the rights you hold.

Information we collect

  • Personal identification. Your name and contact details when you reach out to us.
  • Case information. The details you share about your legal matter.
  • Usage data. Basic, anonymised analytics about how the site is used.
  • Communication records. Our correspondence with you.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and provide legal information.
  • To match you with suitable volunteer counsel.
  • To follow up on your matter.
  • To improve our services and resources.
  • To meet any legal or regulatory obligations.

Data protection & confidentiality

  • Confidentiality. Your case information is treated as confidential.
  • Security. We take reasonable measures to protect your data.
  • Retention. We keep information only as long as necessary.
  • No sale of data. We never sell your information.

Your rights

Under the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Request deletion of your data.

Contact us

Art x Law Initiative Malaysia (ALIM). For any privacy request, email artxlawinitiative@gmail.com.
