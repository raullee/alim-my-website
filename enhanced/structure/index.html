Two streams, one clinic.

The 17 May AGM agreed in principle on a two-stream model: policy / advocacy running on a quarterly cadence, and day-to-day intake running on a weekly cadence, with a shared infrastructure layer underneath. This is a working draft for Nanda to iterate on. Open slots are marked in red.

Cadence: quarterly · Output: position papers, judicial briefs, ministry engagement

Open

How a query moves through

From DM to closure.

01

Intake

Form on alim.my, WhatsApp to the clinic line, or referral from a partner body (PROFIMA, Karyawan, etc.). Logged with a file number.

02

Triage (within 48 hrs)

Ops Lead classifies: pro-se workable, needs counsel, or strategic (policy implication).

03

Routing

→ Pro-se tool (Small Claim Drafter etc.), → matched volunteer counsel from roster, or → escalated to Stream A if it could move policy.

04

First reply (within 14 days)

Substantive first response. Arts Law Centre of Australia SLA adopted as benchmark.

05

Follow-through

Counsel and intake correspondent owns the file to closure or referral. Status tracked weekly.

06

De-identified data → Stream A

Closed files anonymised; patterns feed the policy roadmap and quarterly dispatch.
