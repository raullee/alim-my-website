Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1 Is my consultation really free?+
Yes. Every consultation is 100% pro-bono. We never charge for advice, and we never will.
Q2 Will my information be kept confidential?+
Yes, always. What you share with us stays between you and your volunteer counsel.
Q3 What kinds of artists do you help?+
All creatives and cultural workers, at any level, from students and hobbyists to working professionals.
Q4 Do I need to be in KL?+
No. We help remotely across Malaysia, wherever you're based.
