About ALIM.

Our purpose

Our Mission

ALIM exists to make legal support reachable for Malaysia's creative community: musicians, visual artists, filmmakers, writers, and cultural workers who rarely have a lawyer on speed dial. We offer confidential advice, plain-language guidance, and a growing network of counsel who understand both the law and the realities of a creative career.

Since 2025, our work has stood on three commitments: advice that meets artists where they are, advocacy that pushes for fairer treatment across the industry, and education that closes the gap between artists and the legal system built to protect them.

Our values

What We Stand For

01

Access to Justice

Legal advice should never depend on ability to pay. We keep our services free and confidential for every artist who needs them.

02

Creative Freedom

We defend the right to make, perform, and publish without undue legal or institutional interference.

03

Community Empowerment

We equip artists with the knowledge to recognise their rights and negotiate from a position of strength.

Common questions

Frequently Asked

01Who can access ALIM's services?+

Any artist or cultural worker in Malaysia, whatever your stage or level, from students and emerging creatives to established professionals.

02What kind of legal issues do you help with?+

Contracts, copyright and other intellectual property matters, performance agreements, freelance and employment disputes, censorship issues, and grant or funding agreements.

03How can I support ALIM's mission?+

Volunteer your time or expertise, partner with us as an organisation, donate once our donation channel opens, or simply spread the word to artists who need us.

04Is my consultation kept confidential?+

Yes, always. Every consultation with ALIM is private and confidential.
