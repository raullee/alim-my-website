Reg. 01 / 2026 Creative Worker Registry

Count me in.

Malaysia is drafting a Gig Workers Act. Platform workers (Grab, foodpanda, Lalamove) are inside. Creative workers — visual artists, musicians, filmmakers, dancers, designers, illustrators, writers, theatre — are not. Yet.

ALIM has been proposed by the community to act as the high-level representative body for the creative sector. To lobby the minister effectively, we need numbers. This is the number.
