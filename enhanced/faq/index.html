About ALIM

01 What is ALIM? +
A non-profit initiative providing free legal advice, policy advocacy, and educational resources to Malaysia's creative communities.
02 Who founded ALIM? +
Fahri Azzat, Raul Lee Bhaskaran, and Nicholas Choong, with a growing network of volunteer counsel.
03 Is ALIM a registered organisation? +
Not yet. ALIM runs as a grassroots initiative led by its co-founders and a volunteer network of counsel. We intend to incorporate as a company limited by guarantee; that has not happened yet, and no page on this site should be read as saying it has.
Legal services

How the advice works

04 Is the legal advice really free? +
Yes. All consultations are 100% pro-bono.
05 Is my consultation confidential? +
Yes. Confidentiality is treated as professional and absolute.
06 What kind of legal issues can you help with? +
Contract disputes, copyright and IP, performance agreements, employment and freelance matters, censorship, grants, and more.
07 Do I need to be a professional artist? +
No. We help hobbyists, emerging artists, students, cultural workers, and professionals alike.
08 Can you help if I am outside Kuala Lumpur? +
Yes. We assist remotely across Malaysia by email and video.
