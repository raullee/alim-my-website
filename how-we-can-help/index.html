What We Offer

Our Services & Engagement Process

Understanding how ALIM supports Malaysia's creative communities.

Core Service

Confidential Legal Consultations

Every engagement begins with a private, one-on-one consultation where we take the time to understand your situation fully. Here's what you can expect:

Case Discussion

Thorough discussion of case facts to understand every relevant detail of your situation.

Rights & Obligations

Explanation of legal rights and obligations so you understand where you stand.

Position Assessment

Assessment of strengths and weaknesses of your position with honest, clear analysis.

Strategic Options

Strategic outline of available options and next steps to move forward with confidence.

Document Services

Contract & Document Review

We review and advise on the contracts and agreements that shape your creative career. Don't sign anything you don't fully understand.

Commission & Work-for-Hire

Agreements for commissioned creative work and employment contracts.

Licensing & Royalty Contracts

Agreements governing how your work is used and how you get paid.

Gallery Representation

Agreements between artists and galleries for exhibition and sales.

Non-Disclosure Agreements

NDAs protecting your confidential creative concepts and business dealings.

Conflict Support

Dispute Resolution Support

When disagreements escalate, we help you navigate the path toward resolution without unnecessary confrontation or expense.

Letters of Demand

Drafting formal letters of demand that clearly communicate your position and the outcomes you seek.

Negotiation Strategy

Advising on negotiation strategies that protect your interests while keeping doors open for resolution.

Mediation Support

Exploring mediation as an alternative to court — a faster, less adversarial path to fair outcomes.

Important Limitations

In the spirit of transparency, there are boundaries to the support we can provide. We believe in being upfront about what we can and cannot do.

We do not provide full legal representation in court proceedings.

We do not handle criminal law, family law, or property law matters.

Our services are advisory in nature — we guide and inform, but the decisions are always yours.

Ready to Talk?

Every creative journey deserves legal clarity.

If you're facing a legal challenge or simply want to understand your rights better, we're here. Consultations are confidential and free.
