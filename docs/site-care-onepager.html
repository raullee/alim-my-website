The proposal in one line

Keeping alim.my alive is
not a one-off job.

The site was built once. Keeping it live, lawful, and funder-ready is a standing job that runs every month, quietly, whether or not anyone is looking. This is what that work actually is, and a fair way to fund it.

01 · The misconception
What it looks like
A static brochure site. Built once, done forever.
What it actually is
A live legal-service surface: intake forms, working tools, and legal content that goes out of date the moment the law moves.
02 · What runs under the hood
01

Live client intake

The consultation form routes real enquiries to the inbox, filters spam, and is monitored so a person in distress does not fall into a void.

02

Legal content that ages

Guides on the Gig Workers Act 2025, contracts, freelancer and performer rights. Statutes and cases change; stale legal guidance is a reputational and liability risk, not a typo.

03

The funder's front door

Assessors from Prince Claus, British Council and Goethe judge ALIM by this site before a single meeting. It has to be current and credible on the day they visit, unannounced.

04

Hosting & infrastructure

Domain, SSL, and servers now run on dedicated infrastructure I provide, replacing the external hosting the initiative used to rent.

05

Security & uptime

Certificate renewals, backups, patching, uptime monitoring, and form spam protection. Invisible until the day one of them fails.

06

Content & reporting

New events, refreshed Instagram, new resources, plus GA4 analytics turned into a plain report the team can actually read.

03 · The cost story
RM 300–400 / mo
Before. Paid to an external host for hosting alone. No maintenance. No content. No one watching it.
Same budget
Now. That spend was cancelled. Redirected, it buys hosting on ALIM-dedicated infrastructure and the standing work above: legal-content currency, a monitored intake, and funder-readiness.

The initiative was never getting a free site. It was paying for dumb hosting. The proposal is to point the money it already spent at something that keeps the site working, not just switched on.
