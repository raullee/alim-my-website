# Closing out the review: allocation, messages, mechanism

The whole library is **9,706 words, about 69 minutes to read**. That is the number that
decides whether this closes. Split four ways it is a single sitting each. Lead with it;
an unbounded ask gets deferred, a 40-minute one does not.

Four bundles, grouped so each reviewer stays in one area of law and builds context once
instead of page-hopping. Raul is the author and does not review his own work; he takes the
flags and the closing call.

| | Bundle | Pages | Steps | Reading | Reviewing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Ownership and deals | 4 | 36 | 11 min | ~30 min |
| **B** | Speech, censorship and police | 6 | 54 | 16 min | ~44 min |
| **C** | Events, performance and getting paid | 4 | 43 | 12 min | ~34 min |
| **D** | Selling, organising and keeping | 5 | 46 | 14 min | ~39 min |

**A. Ownership and deals.** Copyright, trademarks, contracts; where an artist's asset is
either protected or signed away.
release-track · sync-licence · someone-used-my-work · management-deal

**B. Speech, censorship and police.** The lane with criminal exposure, and the one where an
error can get somebody charged. Give this to whoever is closest to criminal or media work.
post-set-online · arrested · documentary · publish-book · busking · mural

**C. Events, performance and getting paid.** Licensing a room, bringing in an act, who your
cast legally are, chasing a fee.
dj-club-night · stage-show · foreign-artist · unpaid

**D. Selling, organising and keeping.** Artist as seller, as an organisation, and as a
custodian of other people's material.
merch-online · exhibit-sell-art · run-organisation · brand-deal · archive

Tracker: `review/review-tracker.csv`, one row per page. Keep it yourself. Do not make the
reviewers use a tool; they reply on WhatsApp and you record it.

## The mechanism, in five parts

**1. Assign, do not invite.** "If we take three or four each" is addressed to nobody and
will be done by nobody. Name the person, name the pages, name the date.

**2. Make the reward the attribution.** Every page can carry "Reviewed by [name], [date]".
The mechanism is already built and prints nothing until someone has actually reviewed. For
a lawyer building a profile in arts law, a named credit on a public legal resource is worth
more than the forty minutes. Say so in the ask.

**3. Exceptions only.** Do not ask for mark-up. Ask for one of two replies per page:
"clear", or a numbered list of what is wrong. Most pages will come back "clear" and the
whole thing collapses to a short list you can act on in an evening.

**4. One deadline, and silence is not consent.** Seven days. If someone does not reply, the
page stays live exactly as it is and simply carries no name. Nothing is blocked by silence,
so nobody can hold the project by doing nothing; and nobody gets attributed for work they
did not do.

**5. One call to close.** 45 minutes, agenda is the flags only, not a page-by-page reading.
Anything unresolved on the call goes to the "under revision" state on the page, which is
honest and visible, rather than being argued to death.

## The failure mode to watch

The risk here is not that someone reviews badly. It is that this never gets sent, or gets
sent and sits. Four bundles, four names, one date, and a reply format that takes ninety
seconds is the whole defence against that. If it has not moved in three days, chase once,
by name, in the group rather than privately.
