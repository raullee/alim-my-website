## 1. Why now

The company limited by guarantee is expected to complete in Q4. Three things should be
on the record before it does, because they are much harder to put right afterwards:

- a **register of directors' interests**, so the company inherits one rather than starting
  to build one after its first transaction;
- a **record of contributions in kind**, so ALIM knows what it actually costs to run and
  can count that against grant match-funding requirements;
- **clarity on what intellectual property the company will own** and what it will merely
  be permitted to use.

Section 221 of the Companies Act 2016 will require a director to declare an interest in a
contract or proposed contract with the company, and section 213 requires all of us to act
in the company's best interest. Neither applies to an unincorporated initiative, which is
precisely why it is easier to start now than to retrofit it.

**Proposed:** that every director files a short declaration of outside interests, and that
the register is reviewed annually. My own entries are below. I am going first because it
is my proposal, not because mine are the only ones.

## 2. MatSelamat: offer of a free licence

**My interest, declared.** MatSelamat is a court document generator for small claims
(Form 198) aimed at Malaysian performing artists. It is owned by Axon Avenue PLT, which
is my company. I would gain from this arrangement, and I am setting out how so the board
can weigh it rather than take my word for it.

**What ALIM would get.** A working Form 198 generator, free, for its own non-commercial
use, in perpetuity. ALIM already publishes a small claims guide and the legislation
library now routes people owed money to the Order 93 procedure. The guide tells someone
what to do; it does not do it for them. This closes that gap without ALIM building or
buying anything.

**What I would get, stated plainly rather than left for someone to notice.** Distribution.
MatSelamat's users are ALIM's constituency and no one else's, so ALIM is realistically the
only channel that makes sense for it. This is not a gift in one direction and I am not
going to present it as one.

**Proposed terms.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Fee | None. Zero, perpetual, non-exclusive, for ALIM's non-commercial use. |
| Ownership | Stays with Axon Avenue PLT. ALIM is licensed, not buying. |
| Branding | ALIM-branded or co-branded, at the board's choice. |
| User data | Stays with ALIM. Axon takes no user data and does no marketing off it. |
| Referrals | 🚨 It must not route users to RLB Advocates or to any firm. See section 94(3)(h) of the Legal Profession Act. |
| Exit | ALIM may stop using it at any time. I may only withdraw it on twelve months' notice, so ALIM is never captured. |
| If ALIM ever charges for it | Then it is a different transaction and comes back to the board, with me abstaining again. Nothing is pre-agreed. |

**Alternative the board should actively consider:** that Axon assigns MatSelamat to ALIM
outright for nothing. It earns no revenue today. If the board would rather own the tool
than depend on a director's company for it, say so and I will do that instead. I am
raising this myself because it is the option that is better for ALIM and worse for me,
and the board should not have to think of it unprompted.

## 3. alim.my: recording what has been contributed, and not claiming it

**This is a recording item, not an invoice. I am not asking to be paid and I am not
seeking to recover anything, now or later.**

I have not previously put the cost of the website to the board, which was an oversight on
my part rather than a decision. It matters for three reasons that have nothing to do with
me: ALIM cannot budget for something it has never costed; a successor cannot take over
something that was never documented; and unrecorded in-kind contribution is match funding
that arts and culture funders will count and that we are currently leaving on the table.

**What exists as at 21 September 2026**

| | |
|---|---|
| Public site | 19 pages, with its own design system |
| Legislation library | 87 pages: 67 Acts, codes and treaties, plus 19 situation guides |
| Full statute texts hosted | 92 PDFs, roughly 200 MB, sourced from the Attorney General's Chambers |
| Supporting tooling | Harvester, page builder and review tooling, about 4,300 lines, re-runnable so the library can be refreshed rather than rebuilt |

**Recurring cash costs**

| Item | Paid by | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| alim.my domain | RLB Advocates & Solicitors | _to complete_ |
| Hosting | _to complete_ | _to complete_ |

🚨 Note that the domain is currently billed to my law firm, not to me personally and not to
ALIM. That is a second related-party item and it should be on the register whatever the
board decides.

**Proposed**

1. That the above is recorded in a contributions register as contributed in kind, with **no
   liability to ALIM, past or future**, and no expectation of reimbursement.
2. That no ringgit valuation is entered now. If a funder requires a match-funding figure, a
   valuation is prepared for that application and approved by the board then. Putting a
   number in the minutes today serves no purpose and invites the wrong reading.
3. That on incorporation the board decides whether ALIM takes the domain and hosting onto
   its own account, or whether the present arrangement continues and is simply minuted.
4. That the board appoints someone other than me to hold the administrative credentials as
   a second pair of hands, so the site is not dependent on one person.
