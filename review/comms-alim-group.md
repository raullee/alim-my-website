## SHORT (WhatsApp group)

Following up on what I took on at the meeting. The legislation stash is built and live:

https://alim.my/legislation/

66 Acts and codes that actually reach the arts scene, full text hosted so nobody has to
fight the AGC portal again. All of it tagged to the three objectives and ten segments, so
Karl and Nanda's framework is now something you can click:
alim.my/legislation/?view=framework

The part I think is the real asset: 19 situations mapped end to end. DJing a club night,
busking, posting a set, chasing a fee that never came, getting shut down at a gig, someone
using your work without asking. Each one lists the law that reaches it in the order it
reaches you, and filters by whether you are the artist, the promoter or the venue.

Try searching "DJing", "MCMC", or "client won't pay".

It has been through a hard internal review across IP, media and constitutional, employment,
and tax and corporate. That pass found and fixed 86 errors, including a few that would have
been embarrassing: we were citing a subsection of the Copyright Act that has been deleted,
naming a collecting society whose declaration has been revoked, and telling people a film
posted online needs Censorship Board approval when s.2(3) of the Film Censorship Act says
the opposite.

What I need now is your eyes on it. The Act numbers and section numbers are machine-checked
against AGC's own text; the plain-English explanations are not counsel-signed. Each
situation is one printable page, e.g. alim.my/legislation/doing/dj-club-night/
If we take three or four each we are through it in a week.

Nothing has been announced publicly. Tell me what is wrong first.
