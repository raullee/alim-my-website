# alim.my running costs: schedule and proposal

**Objective:** that I am not out of pocket carrying ALIM's infrastructure, from here forward.

**The distinction the whole thing turns on.** There are two kinds of cost and they have to be
proposed separately, because one is straightforward and the other is not.

- **Costs that exist only because ALIM exists.** The domain, the hosting, the email on the
  domain. If ALIM folded tomorrow these stop. Properly reimbursable, and better still,
  ALIM should simply hold the accounts so no money passes through a director at all.
- **Costs I would carry anyway.** My AI subscription, my laptop, my internet. I pay for
  these for RLB, for Axon, and for my own projects. ALIM's existence did not cause them.
  A charity funding a director's general-purpose tooling is a different proposition and it
  will be read as one.

Everything below is at **cost, with receipts, no margin, no charge for my time.**

**Note on the hosting figure.** alim.my currently sits on my Hostinger Premium plan
alongside raul.com.my and others. A Premium plan carries many sites, so ALIM's marginal
cost of being on mine is close to nothing. **I am therefore not proposing to invoice ALIM
a share of my plan**, because that would be charging ALIM more than it actually costs me.
The clean version is that ALIM takes its own entry-level Hostinger plan in its own name.
That gives an exact, auditable, attributable figure, and it means the site does not live
inside a director's personal account, which matters for succession more than it matters
for money.

**The RM400 is the anchor and it should be said first.** ALIM was already spending RM400 a
year on hosting. Fahri was paying it. Earlier this year I told him to cancel it because I
had picked it up, so that line went to zero. **I am not asking ALIM to take on a new cost.
I am asking it to resume a line it already had, in its own name, at what it actually
costs.** On any likely plan that is at or below the RM400 it was already paying.

**Proposed**
1. ALIM opens its own hosting account and registers or transfers alim.my to ALIM, on
   incorporation or sooner.
2. Until that happens, I keep carrying it and it is minuted as in kind.
3. I am **not claiming arrears** for the period I have carried it. That is contributed and
   closed.
4. The domain moves off RLB's billing. It should not sit with my law firm.

## B. Tooling: what I am not asking for, and why I am saying so myself

My Anthropic subscription is **USD 108 a month, about USD 1,296 a year**, roughly
**RM 5,400** at 4.20 to the dollar. Update the rate before anyone relies on it.

The legislation library was built with it, and keeping the library current needs it: the
harvester has to be re-run, consolidation dates drift, and the scenario pages need
maintaining. So the tooling is genuinely load-bearing for ALIM's work.

**I am still not proposing that ALIM pays it, for three reasons.**

1. **I would be paying it anyway.** It runs RLB's work and Axon's. ALIM did not cause the
   cost, and a cost you would incur regardless is not one to push onto a charity.
2. **The proportion is wrong.** RM5,400 a year of AI tooling in an organisation whose
   entire previous infrastructure spend was RM400 would be the largest line in the budget
   and it would be paid to a director. That is the first thing a funder queries in an
   acquittal and the first thing a journalist notices.
3. **The CLBG constitution will very likely forbid it.** A company limited by guarantee
   normally bars applying funds for the benefit of a director, with a carve-out for
   reimbursement of expenses properly incurred on the company's behalf. Hosting is squarely
   inside that carve-out. A shared general-purpose subscription is not obviously inside it,
   and I would rather not find that out after the fact.

**What I propose instead, in order of preference**

1. **Record it, do not reimburse it.** The subscription goes in the contributions register
   as in kind, with no liability to ALIM. If a funder asks for match funding, we value the
   ALIM share then and it becomes money in our favour rather than money out.
2. **If ALIM later needs its own,** it buys its own seat at the tier ALIM's work actually
   needs, in ALIM's name, and I use it only for ALIM. That is an ALIM asset, not a
   reimbursement to me, and it is defensible to anyone.
3. **Put it on the commercial arm, not the charity.** We discussed merch, courses, and
   managing selected cases for a fee. Tooling to produce courses and run a case pipeline
   sits naturally with the commercial vehicle, which can pay properly for it once it earns.
   That is the honest route to my being fully covered, and it does not lean on the
   charitable side at all.

## C. What I am asking the board to decide

1. That ALIM opens its own hosting account and takes alim.my into its own name, restoring
   the RM400 line it already had, at actual cost.
2. That the domain comes off RLB's billing.
3. That a contributions register is opened, that my carrying of hosting to date and my
   tooling are recorded in it as contributed with **no liability to ALIM**, and that every
   director files their own entries.
4. That someone other than me holds a second set of administrative credentials, so the site
   is not dependent on one person.
5. That tooling is revisited only when the commercial arm exists and earns, not before.

I have an interest in items 1, 2 and 3 and will abstain.
