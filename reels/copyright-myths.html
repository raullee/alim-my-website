a license to use
You still need permission from the creator
04
MYTH

Sharing online does NOT
give up your rights
Your Instagram post is still YOUR intellectual property
05
MYTH
"Copyright lasts
forever"
Life of author + 50 years
in Malaysia
After that, works enter the public domain
