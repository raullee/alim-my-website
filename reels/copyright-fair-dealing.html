3 real scenarios.

SCENARIO 1
Music reviewer uses 30s of a song in a monetized YouTube review
"I only used 30 seconds and I'm reviewing the song, so it's fair use right?"
FAIR DEALING METER
DEPENDS ON CONTEXT
Monetization + using the "heart" of the song tips the scale. Commentary helps but doesn't guarantee protection.
SCENARIO 2
Lecturer photocopies an entire journal article for 40 students
"It's for education, so it must be fair dealing."
FAIR DEALING METER
LIKELY NOT FAIR DEALING
Copying the entire work for 40 people exceeds reasonable educational use under Malaysian law.
SCENARIO 3
Someone turns your artwork into a viral Twitter meme
"It's just a meme bro, chill. It's free publicity."
FAIR DEALING METER
NOT FAIR DEALING
Using the full work without permission, transformation, or commentary. "Free publicity" is not a legal defense.
